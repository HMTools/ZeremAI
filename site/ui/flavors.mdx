The **Flavors** screen lists every flavor you have: what you like and dislike, in your own words. You edit them here, with no file to open. See [Flavors](/guide/flavors) for what a flavor is and how it differs from a skill.

## What is on the screen

1. **Add flavor** opens the form above the list.
2. **Waiting for you.** One amber inbox for every flavor that a flow learned. Each proposal has a label (`New`, `Change` or `Forget`), the topic, the text, and where it came from: the flow name, the time and, when the flow saved them, the words you said. A change shows the old text crossed out, then the new one. The tab shows how many proposals wait.
3. **Your flavors.** Grouped by topic, newest first. Above the list are one chip for each topic (with a count) and a search box. The search reads the topic and the text.
4. **One row for each flavor** shows the text and where it came from: `You wrote it`, or `Learned from <flow name>`, with the time.

## The buttons

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Add flavor** | Opens a form: **Topic** (type one, or tap one of your topics) and **What you prefer** (one clear sentence, 160 characters at most). **What a step sees** shows the exact lines a step would get. **Save flavor** applies it at once. **Ctrl or ⌘ + Enter** saves too. |
| **Edit** | The text of the row becomes a field. **Save** or **Enter** keeps it. **Cancel** or **Esc** drops the change. The row then shows `✓ Saved just now`. |
| **Delete** | Deletes at once, with no question. The row leaves the list and a toast shows **Undo** for 6 seconds. **Undo** puts it back and nothing is deleted. After 6 seconds the delete is sent. If you close the page in those 6 seconds, the flavor stays. |
| **Accept** | On a proposal. A new flavor becomes active. A change gives the flavor its new text. A "forget" deletes the flavor. Steps use the result from the next step on. |
| **Reject** | On a proposal. Deletes the proposal and changes nothing else. |
| **Accept all** | In the inbox, when two or more proposals wait. Accepts every one, oldest first, in one pass. A proposal whose flavor is gone is dropped, and the answer says how many were skipped. |

Only a flavor with status active reaches a step. A proposal that waits does not.

## When you have no flavors

The screen offers three examples to add in one tap, and one line on the difference between a flavor and a skill.

## On a phone

Rows stack: the text, then the buttons under it, each at least 44 px high. The inbox shows the first proposal, with **Show the other N** for the rest. **Add flavor** stays at the bottom of the screen.
