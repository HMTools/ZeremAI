The **Flows** screen is where you spend most of your time. It opens on an **overview** of every flow. Pick a flow to see its steps.

## The overview

  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/ui/flows-overview-dark.png" alt="The overview: four counts at the top, a card for each flow that needs you, a card for a running flow with the line of what it does now, and a short list of paused and done flows." />

1. **The counts.** How many flows need you, run, are paused and are done.
2. **Needs you.** One card for each flow that waits for an approval, is blocked or has failed. The card has the buttons: **Approve**, **Request changes** or **Continue**. You do not need to open the flow.
3. **Running.** One card for each flow that works. A bar shows its steps, and a line shows the last thing the agent did, for example `▸ Edit refund-service.ts · 12 s ago`. If nothing happened for 10 minutes, the line turns amber and says `No activity for 14 min`.
4. **Paused and done.** One row for each. **Continue** resumes a paused flow.

**here** marks a flow that your session runs. **Open flow** shows the steps of a flow. **← All flows** returns to the overview.

## One flow

<Frame caption="One flow in Diagram view. The steps are one column of boxes, and an approval step has a You approve tag. Demo data.">
  <img className="dark:hidden" src="/images/ui/flows-waiting-light.png" alt="One flow in Diagram view: the header with a bar of phases, a card for step 3 that waits for approval, and the steps as one column of boxes grouped by phase. The step that waits has a You approve tag." />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/ui/flows-waiting-dark.png" alt="One flow in Diagram view: the header with a bar of phases, a card for step 3 that waits for approval, and the steps as one column of boxes grouped by phase. The step that waits has a You approve tag." />
</Frame>

1. **The header.** Its name, which session runs it (`Runs in:`), its state, the step it is on, its template and the time of its last change. A bar shows the phases and the steps, with the count of steps done. For a running flow, the activity line is here too. The switch on the right picks **List** or **Diagram**.
2. **The card for a step that needs you.** It names the step and says why. For an approval it has **Approve** and **Request changes**. **Show the step** jumps to the step. See [Approvals](/guide/approvals).
3. **The buttons of the flow.** **Close flow** and **Delete flow**, described below.
4. **The steps.** In **Diagram** (above) the steps are one column of boxes grouped by phase, with an arrow from each step to the next. A step that you approve has a **You approve** tag, so you see where you step in. In **List** the steps are a list. Steps that are done fold into one line. Tap **Show** to open them.
5. **The step panel.** Tabs **Step**, **Logs**, **Memory** and **Files** show what the flow did, what it remembers and the files it changed. Only you, the owner, can read the last three.

Each step has a symbol and a word: `waits for you`, `blocked`, `running`, `done`, `failed`, `paused` or `pending`. Colour is never the only signal.

## What you can do

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Approve** | The same click as in the approval dialog. The step is marked approved and can complete. |
| **Request changes** | Opens a box, **What should change?**. Write the reason, then press **Send changes**, or **Cancel**. The agent reads it, changes the step and asks again. |
| **Continue** | Resumes a paused, pending, failed or blocked flow from its last incomplete step. It also shows for a running flow that no live session holds (for example after you resumed Claude Code, which gives the session a new id), and for a flow whose agent in this session has been silent for 10 minutes. Above it, **Add a note for the agent** opens a box for an optional note that goes with **Continue**. If the flow ran in another project folder, you see that folder first and press **Continue anyway** or **Keep it**. A step that was in progress is not set again: the agent checks its output, asks for approval again if the output is complete, or runs the step again. A child flow comes back with its running parents, and the child answers first. |
| **Take over** | Shows for a running flow that another live session holds. Press **Yes, take over** to confirm. This session binds the flow, and the other session stops working on it. |
| **Close flow** | Asks you to confirm with **Yes, close it** or **Keep it**. Your session stops working on that flow. The files stay, and you can **Continue** later. |
| **Delete flow** | Asks `Delete "<name>"? Its folder is removed for good.` Press **Yes, delete it** or **Keep it**. It then removes the flow folder. It works on any active flow, also one that another session runs. |

## A blocked step

A blocked step shows a card with the step name, the reason in one sentence, the command to run when there is one, and **Continue**. There is nothing to approve. Do what the reason says, then press **Continue**. See [Blocked steps](/guide/blocked-steps).

A step that starts child flows says `Starts 3 child flows` in the diagram. A card for each child hangs under that step. It shows the name of the child, its state, its bar and its own step line. Six cards show first. Press **Show all N**, where N is the number of children, for the rest. Tap a card to open that child. In List view, the step says `Runs 3 child flows` and links to each. A child is a flow of its own, and it shows in the overview with its own line of activity. See [Sub-flows](/guide/sub-flows).

## Light and dark

The round button at the right end of the top bar sets the theme. Each press goes to the next state: **auto** (follows your computer), **light**, **dark**. The mod saves your pick in `~/.claude/zeremai/config/ui.json`, so every page of every session opens in it. A person who only views the page sees your pick and has no button.
