The **Flows** screen is where you spend most of your time. It shows every flow, with the flows that need you first.

## What is on the screen

1. **The flow row.** One button for each flow, with its state word and its age. **here** marks a flow that your session runs. Pick a flow to see it below.
2. **The header of the flow.** Its name, which session runs it (`Runs in:`), its state, the step it is on, its template and the time of its last change. The switch on the right picks the view of the steps.
3. **The card for a step that needs you.** It names the step and says why. For an approval it has **Approve** and **Request changes**. **Show the step** jumps to the step. See [Approvals](/guide/approvals).
4. **The buttons of the flow.** **Close flow** and **Delete flow**, described below.
5. **The steps.** In **Two lanes** (above) Claude is on the left and you are on the right, so you see when Claude works alone and when you step in. In **Runbook** the steps are a list.
6. **The step panel.** Tabs **Step**, **Logs**, **Memory** and **Files** show what the flow did, what it remembers and the files it changed. Only you, the owner, can read the last three.

Each step has a symbol and a word: `waits for you`, `blocked`, `running`, `done`, `failed`, `paused` or `pending`. Colour is never the only signal.

## What you can do

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Approve** | The same click as in the approval dialog. The step is marked approved and can complete. |
| **Request changes** | Needs a reason. The agent reads it, changes the step and asks again. |
| **Continue** | Resumes a paused, failed or blocked flow from its last incomplete step. |
| **Close flow** | Asks you to confirm. Your session stops working on that flow. The files stay, and you can **Continue** later. |
| **Delete flow** | Asks `Delete "<name>"? Its folder is removed for good.` and then removes the flow folder. It works on any active flow, also one that another session runs. |

## A blocked step

A blocked step shows a card with the step name, the reason in one sentence, the command to run when there is one, and **Continue**. There is nothing to approve. Do what the reason says, then press **Continue**. See [Blocked steps](/guide/blocked-steps).

## A flow with child flows

A step that starts a child flow shows a link to it. In Runbook it reads `Runs a child flow. Open "<name>"`. The child is a flow of its own in the flow row. See [Sub-flows](/guide/sub-flows).
