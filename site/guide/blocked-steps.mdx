---
title: "Blocked steps"
description: "What it means when a step is blocked, what to do, and how the flow goes on."
---

A step is **blocked** when a retry cannot help and you must act first. Typical causes are an expired login, no access, or a missing secret.

## What you see

- The site shows a card for the step: `Step 3 · <name> is blocked`, with the reason in one sentence and, when there is one, the command to run. The card has a **Continue** button.
- The button above the prompt counts the step as one that needs you, in the same group as `approve`, `failed` and `paused`.
- The agent tells you the reason and the command in the terminal, then ends its turn.

A blocked step has nothing to approve. It is not an approval gate.

## What to do

1. Do what the reason says, for example run the command it names.
2. Press **Continue** on [the site](/guide/live-site), or next to the button above the prompt.

The step runs again from its start.

## What the agent cannot do

- Complete a blocked step. `update_step_status` refuses `completed`.
- Start a later step while an earlier one is blocked.
- Block a step without a reason. The reason is required, and the command is optional.

Any other status on the step clears the block.

## For template authors

You do not mark a step blocked in a template. The step executor reports it when it meets a problem that a retry cannot fix. See [`update_step_status`](/reference/tools#progress).
