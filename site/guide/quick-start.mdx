## 1. Open the pane

```
/zeremai
```

It opens a pane with a **New** tab (start a flow) and a **Continue** tab (resume one).

## 2. Create a flow

On the **New** tab:

1. Pick a template.
2. Fill in its fields. Required ones have a star.
3. Press **Create**.

ZeremAI resolves the template's steps, substitutes your parameters, creates the flow in `~/.claude/zeremai/active/`, binds it to your session and starts the first step.

## 3. Work through the steps

For each step the agent:

1. Marks it `in_progress`.
2. Hands it to a **step-executor** subagent that follows the step's guidance and writes results to flow memory.
3. If the step needs approval, shows you its output in a dialog. You click **Approve** or **Request changes**.
4. Marks it `completed` and moves on.

You can talk to the agent at any time. Every request or correction you make is logged in the flow's audit log.

## 4. Finish

Every flow ends with two automatic steps:

- **Continuous Improvement**: saves findings about what could be better. See [Continuous improvement](/guide/improvements).
- **Complete Flow**: deletes leftover artifact files, then moves the flow to `completed/`.

## Resume later

Open `/zeremai` → **Continue**. Each active flow shows its current step and last change. Pick one and it resumes from the last incomplete step.

## Tips

- Be specific in parameters. Vague input gives vague plans.
- Review each step's output before approving.
- Never hand-edit the flow's state files. ZeremAI refuses agent edits to them, and your own edit can corrupt the flow.
