## Why ZeremAI

- **Reusable templates**: define a workflow once with parameters and ordered steps.
- **State that persists**: close the session, come back, resume at the exact step.
- **User-only approvals**: the agent cannot approve its own work.
- **Context isolation**: each step runs in its own subagent.
- **Shareable marketplaces**: install templates from git repos, public or private.
- **Self-improving**: every flow ends by saving findings you can review later.
