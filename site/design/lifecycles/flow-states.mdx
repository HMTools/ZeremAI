## A flow

A flow has one of four statuses. The status decides which folder the flow lives in.

<Frame caption="States of a flow">
  <img className="dark:hidden" src="/images/diagrams/flow-status-light.svg" alt="Diagram: States of a flow" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/diagrams/flow-status-dark.svg" alt="Diagram: States of a flow" />
</Frame>

| Status | Folder |
|---|---|
| `running`, `paused` | `~/.claude/zeremai/active/<flow>/` |
| `completed` | `~/.claude/zeremai/completed/<flow>/` |
| `failed` | `~/.claude/zeremai/failed/<flow>/` |

`update-flow-status` changes the status and moves the folder in one command. It writes the new status into `flow.json` first. Then it checks the target folder, moves the folder, and writes the new `flowPath` into `flow.json`. The answer has `relocated_to`: the agent must use that path from then on. If the target folder already exists, the move fails, but the new status is already written. The flow stays in its old folder with the new status.

## How a flow starts

`create-flow` reads the template and builds the flow:

1. It copies each step definition into `flow.json`, so a flow does not change if its template changes later. See [Template sources](/design/template-sources).
2. It adds the framework steps (Complete Flow, then Continuous Improvement) after the template steps.
3. It writes three files: `flow.json`, `log.json` and `memory/_cross-references.json`.

The mod then binds the folder to the session. The first step starts as `pending`.

## A step

  <img className="dark:hidden" src="/images/diagrams/step-status-light.svg" alt="Diagram: States of a step" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/diagrams/step-status-dark.svg" alt="Diagram: States of a step" />
</Frame>

This is the normal path. The script accepts any of the six statuses (`pending`, `in_progress`, `completed`, `skipped`, `awaiting_approval`, `blocked`) and sets `startedAt` and `completedAt` for you.

A step is `blocked` when a retry cannot help and you must act first. The agent must give a `reason`, and may give a `command`. The script refuses `completed` on a blocked step. It also refuses to start a step (`in_progress`) while any earlier step is neither `completed` nor `skipped`. A blocked step is one case of this rule. You press **Continue** and the step runs again. Any other status clears the block. The diagram above shows the normal path only. See [Blocked steps](/guide/blocked-steps). It has one rule of its own. A step with `approvalRequired: true` adds a check on the `in_progress → completed` arrow: the script refuses it until `approved` is `true`. Only your click sets `approved`. See [The approval gate](/design/guards/approval-gate).

`approved` is a flag on the step, not a status. A step waiting for you is `in_progress`, with `approvalRequired` and no `approved`. The status `awaiting_approval` is allowed, and the site shows it as "waits for you", but the gate does not depend on it.

The agent sets `in_progress` **before** it starts the `step-executor` subagent. The stop guard reads this status to know that a subagent works the step. See [Stop prevention](/design/guards/stop-prevention).

## Sub-flows

A step with a `subFlowTrigger` starts a child flow.

<Frame caption="A sub-flow reports to its parent">
  <img className="dark:hidden" src="/images/diagrams/subflow-report-light.svg" alt="Diagram: A sub-flow reports to its parent" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/diagrams/subflow-report-dark.svg" alt="Diagram: A sub-flow reports to its parent" />
</Frame>

- The child is a normal flow in `active/`. The mod binds it after its parent, and the newest bound flow answers first.
- A child cannot complete without a `summary`. The script refuses `completed` for a sub-flow with no summary.
- The report to the parent is best effort. If the parent cannot be told, the child still completes.
- The summary goes into the parent's memory, in the topic `sub-flows`, under the step that started the child.

## Loops

A flow goes back to an earlier step with the script command `route-step`. It sets the steps from the target to the current step to `pending`, clears their `approved`, `startedAt` and `completedAt`, and keeps their memory and log. Nothing else moves a finished step: `update-step-status` refuses to reopen a `completed` step.

| Who | How | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| You | A Back button on an approval step (`routes`, or `loopBackTo` as one route). You give a reason. | None. The count of automatic loops of the steps that restart goes back to 0. |
| A check | The step has `onVerifierFail` and a check still fails after the fix tries (`maxFixes`). The mod runs `route-step` with `by: "verifier"`. | `maxIterations`, 3 when none is named. |
| The agent | `route-step` with `by: "retry"`, to the `loopBackTo` of the step. The agent decides when (`loopCondition`). | The same count and limit. |

The count is `loopsUsed` on the step. When it reaches the limit, the script refuses, the step becomes `blocked`, and you decide. **Request changes** also sets the count of that step to 0 (`reset-loops`). The mod alone runs the click and the failed-check forms, and `reset-loops`. See [Write a template](/authoring/templates).
